Free Roam Lesson

A small SkyKit viewer: streamed Gaia stars, keyboard navigation, status, and a browser debug bridge.

  • WASD or arrows move through parsecs.
  • Drag in the viewer to look around.
  • Q/E move down/up.
  • Shift boosts movement.
  • Try skykitDebug.snapshot() in the console.
